Finn Law Group: what to know

By Andrea · Last updated: August 2026

If you're researching Finn Law Group, you probably want a clear, honest picture before you spend any money. This page is a neutral overview and, more importantly, a plain-English guide to how to evaluate any timeshare law firm before you hire. What Finn Law Group appears to be

Finn Law Group is generally described as a US law firm that has represented timeshare owners in disputes with resort developers. Based on how the firm is commonly referenced online, its work touches areas like timeshare contracts, consumer protection, and related disputes.

We're keeping this description general on purpose. Firm details, staff, practice areas, and licensing can change over time. Before you rely on anything, confirm the current details on the firm's own website and check the attorneys' licensing on the relevant state bar. Do not treat any summary, including ours, as a substitute for your own verification.

What a firm like this may help with

A law firm that focuses on timeshare matters may be able to help with issues such as:

  • Fraud or misrepresentation claims, if you believe you were misled during the sales presentation.
  • Contract disputes, including questions about how your agreement was formed or enforced.
  • Negotiations with a developer or resort on your behalf.

These are the same kinds of situations we cover in more detail on our timeshare lawyer page. Whether a lawyer is the right fit depends on your specific contract, your state, and the facts of your case. A firm cannot promise what it can achieve until it reviews your situation.

How to evaluate any timeshare law firm

Use the same careful checklist for any firm you're considering, including this one:

  • Verify licensing. Confirm the attorneys are licensed and in good standing with the state bar where they practice. State bar websites let you look this up for free.
  • Get it in writing. Look for a clear, written engagement letter and fee agreement that spells out the scope of work and what you're paying for.
  • Be cautious about large upfront fees. Big payments before any work is done, especially paired with a guarantee, are a common warning sign across the exit industry.
  • Never trust a guarantee. No firm can guarantee your timeshare will be canceled. Outcomes depend on facts and law.
  • Read independent reviews. Look beyond a company's own marketing. Check multiple neutral sources.
  • Compare with free options first. Before paying anyone, make sure you've exhausted cheaper and free routes.

A note on upfront fees and guarantees. A promise that your timeshare will be canceled, combined with a large payment before any work happens, is a pattern regulators warn consumers about. A legitimate professional will explain risks honestly and won't guarantee a result.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. Which attorney will handle my matter, and are they licensed and in good standing in my state?
  2. What exactly will you do for me, and what is the written scope of work?
  3. What are your total fees, how are they structured, and what is refundable if things don't work out?
  4. Do you require money upfront, and what happens to it if my case stalls?
  5. What outcomes are realistic for a contract and situation like mine, and what are the risks?
  6. Can you share references or independent reviews I can verify on my own?

Frequently asked questions

Is Finn Law Group legitimate?

We can't make that judgment for you, and it wouldn't be fair to assert it either way. The responsible step is to verify for yourself. Check the attorneys' licensing and good standing with the state bar, review the firm's profile with the Better Business Bureau, and read independent reviews from more than one source. The FTC's consumer site at consumer.ftc.gov also explains common timeshare and exit-industry warning signs.

How much does a timeshare lawyer cost?

It varies widely depending on the firm, your state, the complexity of your case, and the fee structure. There is no standard price. Always get the full cost and terms in a written fee agreement before you agree to anything.

Can a law firm guarantee my timeshare will be canceled?

No. No firm can honestly guarantee that your timeshare will be canceled. Outcomes depend on the facts of your case and the law that applies. Treat any guarantee as a reason to slow down and ask more questions.

How do I check a law firm before hiring?

Start with the state bar to confirm each attorney is licensed and in good standing. Check the BBB and independent reviews, ask for a written engagement and fee agreement, and be cautious about large upfront payments. For background on when a lawyer even makes sense, see our timeshare lawyer page.
