Contact us

We are an information site, and we love hearing from readers — especially with corrections, updated details about a resort or program, or suggestions for topics to cover.

Email us

The best way to reach us is by email: contact@timeshareunlocked.com. We read every message, though we cannot always reply individually.

Please note: Timeshare Unlocked is not a law firm, an exit company, or a resort, and we cannot give personal legal or financial advice, review your contract, or contact your developer on your behalf. For advice about your specific situation, please speak with a licensed attorney in your state and confirm details with your resort/developer and the FTC (consumer.ftc.gov).

What we can help with

  • Corrections or outdated information on any of our pages.
  • Suggestions for new guides or questions you wish we answered.
  • Feedback on how to make the site clearer or more useful.

What we cannot do

  • Give legal, financial, or tax advice about your personal situation.
  • Cancel a timeshare, negotiate with a developer, or represent you.
  • Recommend a specific exit company or law firm to hire.
