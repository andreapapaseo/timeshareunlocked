Privacy policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Timeshare Unlocked ("we," "us," this site) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to timeshareunlocked.com. Last updated: August 2026.

Information we collect

Timeshare Unlocked is an information website. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information. Two types of data may be collected:

  • Information you send us. If you email us at contact@timeshareunlocked.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, so we can respond.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics and advertising partners may automatically log technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and the pages you view. This may be collected using cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners may use them to understand how the site is used, to remember your preferences, and to support advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

Advertising

This site is supported by advertising. We may use third-party advertising companies, including Google, to serve ads when you visit. These companies may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and manage your ad settings, at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and myadcenter.google.com.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising from many providers at optout.aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.

Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand aggregate traffic (for example, which pages are most read). This helps us improve the guides. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not used to identify you personally.

Your privacy choices

Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland: Where required, we present a consent choice for advertising and analytics cookies before they are set, and you can change your choice at any time.

Visitors in California and other U.S. states: Depending on your state, you may have rights to access, delete, or opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. Where a consent or opt-out choice is required, it is made available through the privacy controls on this site, and you can also use the industry opt-out links above.

Data retention and security

We keep email correspondence only as long as needed to respond and to keep records of corrections. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@timeshareunlocked.com. For general consumer guidance about timeshares and scams, see the FTC at consumer.ftc.gov.
